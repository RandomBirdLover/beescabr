WHAT THIS FOLDER IS
===================

Who surveyed, when, and where. This is the folder that turns a pile of
observations into "a survey somebody did on a date along a transect".

Writes to data/project_info/.


THE FOLDERS
===========

They mirror data/project_info/, so a script sits where its output does:

    rosters/    the people roster and what is derived from it
    surveys/    the survey record: dates, calendars, transects
    review/     the QC files a person looks at

At this level sit finding_project_info.R, the orchestrator that builds the
master survey record and writes into all three of those, and
collect_plant_names.R, which gathers plant names into the crosswalk.


THE PEOPLE
==========

  rosters/build_people_roster.R   ONE ROW PER HUMAN, with a stable person_id.
  rosters/build_participation.R   who actually took part, DERIVED from the surveys.

Those two are separate on purpose. The roster is declared: it says a person
exists and how to spell their name. Participation is derived: it says what they
actually did. The old roster mixed the two and recorded a volunteer as having
surveyed six times when the record showed none.

A person is joined by person_id, never by name. Names appear in three forms in
this project -- "Sam O'Dell" in a log, "S O'Dell" on a museum label, a handle on
iNaturalist -- and two people share a first name.


THE SURVEYS
===========

  finding_project_info.R                       builds the master survey record
  surveys/finding_beeple_calendar.R            parses the volunteer calendar PDFs
  surveys/finding_survey_dates.R               when each survey happened
  surveys/resolve_beeple_transects_per_survey.R  which transect each survey covered

review/ writes the QC files: date windows that do not line up, transects that
overlap, iNat tags that matched no survey. Questions for a person, not errors.
