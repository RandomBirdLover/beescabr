WHAT THIS FOLDER IS
===================

Builds the species lists: which bees have been recorded, and on what evidence.

Writes to data/checklists/.


THE FILES
=========

  checklist_build.R      the shared builder every list below uses
  cabr_bee_checklist.R   Cabrillo -- THE ONE THAT MATTERS
  pl_bee_checklist.R     Point Loma
  sd_bee_checklist.R     San Diego County

cabr_bee_checklist.R produces cabr_official_native_bee_checklist_generated.csv,
which is the answer to "what bees are at Cabrillo". Cite that one and no other.

It is the union of the iNaturalist list and the specimen list, one row per
taxon, with columns saying which source each came from. A taxon backed by a
specimen is settled; one backed only by a photograph rests on somebody's
identification.

The county list is a CROSS-CHECK. It never adds a species -- it only tells you
whether the county authority also has it.
