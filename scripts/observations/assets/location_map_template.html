How to fix a flagged pin

@@HOWNOTE@@
For each pin, ask: “Was I actually surveying that transect right there?” Open the observation (click its dot → the iNaturalist link), then pick A or B.

A Pin is in the WRONG place

You were on the transect — GPS just dropped the pin off it. On iNaturalist: Edit → drag the marker onto the transect → Save. Keep all your tags.

B Pin is in the RIGHT place

Correct spot, but it wasn’t a transect survey — it got tagged by mistake. On iNaturalist: Edit, remove the Cabrillo…BeeSurvey / CabrilloBee10MinuteSurvey hashtag and the “Bee Survey Transect Name” field (or a #TP-style tag), then Save. Don’t move the pin.

Surveyed, but tagged the wrong transect?

Don’t remove anything — just fix the “Bee Survey Transect Name” field to the correct one (TP / BST / UPMON / OT).
