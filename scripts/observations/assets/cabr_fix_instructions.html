Fixing your flagged survey pins 🐝

Cabrillo National Monument bee monitoring · location cleanup on iNaturalist

You got a map with some of your iNaturalist observations flagged ( bee, plant). Their location pin landed more than 50 m from any survey transect. Nothing has been deleted — we just want the map to be accurate. For each flagged pin, ask one question and pick A or B.

Ask yourself: “Was I actually doing a transect survey right there?”

Open the observation (use the link on your map), then decide:

A The pin is in the WRONG place

You were on the transect — iNaturalist just dropped the pin off it (a GPS glitch). → Move the pin onto the transect.

  1. On the observation page, click Edit.
  2. On the location map, drag the marker to where you actually were on the transect (or type the correct coordinates).
  3. Click Save. Leave all your tags and fields exactly as they are.

B The pin is in the RIGHT place

The location is correct — you just weren’t on a transect (a personal / opportunistic sighting), and it picked up a survey tag by mistake. → Remove the survey tag(s). Don’t move the pin.

  1. On the observation page, click Edit.
  2. In Tags, remove the Cabrillo survey hashtag — e.g. Cabrillo2024BeeSurvey, Cabrillo2025BeeSurvey, or CabrilloBee10MinuteSurvey (whatever’s on it).
  3. Scroll to Observation Fields and remove “Bee Survey Transect Name” (TP/BST/UPMON/OT) if it’s there — plus any #TP / #BST-style transect tag.
  4. Click Save. The observation stays on iNaturalist — it just won’t count as a transect survey.

Edge case: if you were surveying but tagged the wrong transect, don’t remove anything — just fix the “Bee Survey Transect Name” field to the correct one (TP / BST / UPMON / OT). If you truly can’t remember, leave it and let Brandi know.

That’s it — no need to tell anyone when you finish. The next data pull picks up your fix and the pin drops off the list. Questions → reach out to Brandi. Thanks for helping keep the survey clean! 🐝
